outcomes and independent uniform 16-card draws. Rows have denominator 9,216.

The IID deck convention does not model deck order, held cards or cash insolvency.
Floating stationary values are checked by residuals; transition counts are exact
integers. The linear solution is independently calculated, not a copied table.
