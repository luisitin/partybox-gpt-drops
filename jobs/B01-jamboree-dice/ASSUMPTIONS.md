# B01 assumptions

Exact odds are conditional on independent, equally likely listed face indices.
Neither Nintendo RNG frequencies nor Luigi activation probabilities are guessed.
Custom Dice choices are deterministic. Coin bonuses are gross immediate rewards,
independent of movement offset, item purchase cost, caps, and board effects.

The sealed independent author received only the original prompt, root README,
the public API contract, and dice.json before writing the reference. Original
production and Python reference history is retained; the new sealed TypeScript
reference supplies the requested blind comparison.

Unresolved research claims retain their confidence and explicit UNVERIFIED status.
Two pages reusing one underlying publication do not count as independent sources.
