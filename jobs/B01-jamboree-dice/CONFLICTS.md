# B01 conflicts and exclusions

This is a partial research drop, not a certification of Nintendo's implementation. IDs resolve to URLs and short quotations in SOURCES.md. No disagreement below is silently merged into an authoritative value.

| ID | Issue | Evidence and treatment |
|---|---|---|
| C01 | Character dice from another game | The request presupposes character-specific and coin-face dice. S01/S02 explicitly describe their removal and the shared 1–10 block in Jamboree. All 22 characters are aliases, not 22 invented special dice. No 2018 character-face data is imported. |
| C02 | Incorrect 1–6 guide | S22 says the normal block is 1–6 and consequently gives incorrect Double/Triple ranges. S01/S02 contradict it. S22 also describes Creepy/Super Creepy effects unlike the item descriptions in S10/S25. S22 is rejected, not counted as corroboration. |
| C03 | Ticket uses: total versus additional | S12/S13 describe two to four uses. S03 describes two to four additional turns. The wording disagrees. Only the per-use effect is modeled; quantity, persistence, expiry and their probabilities remain unresolved. |

S05 explicitly carries a Superstars rule into Jamboree; S06 uses an inheritance chain through earlier games. Those chains are weaker than directly inspecting Jamboree footage. S27 now independently confirms the +10 doubles reward. The ordinary Triple Dice 20-or-50 amounts now have direct independent Namu corroboration alongside the direct MarioWiki Jamboree table. Turbo 30-or-70 and Payday-specific matching observations remain single-family reports. We do not treat another game's table as independent confirmation. |
| C05 | Mirror is not independent | S23 discloses reuse of S07. Both belong to the same provenance family. MarioWiki article-to-article links, aliases and cached copies also count as one family, not two. S23's second full retrieval failed; see VERIFY.md. |
| C06 | Turbo marketing versus passing events | S15 says Turbo can help reach the Star, while S14/S03 explain that passing interactions are skipped. | C07 | Misleading item-stacking wording | S15 contains loose advice about using items in a turn. The package never emits fictional 2d3, 3d3 or Double-Custom game items. Further item-stacking permutations are not certified. |
| C08 | Incorrect triple probability | S24 gives 1/10000 for any three equal rolls. In the explicit independent-uniform 1–10 model, there are 10 equal triples among 1000 ordered outcomes, so the correct fraction is 1/100. A specified triple such as three sevens is 1/1000. This is a mathematical contradiction, not a disputed game observation. |
| C09 | Together matching reward missed by initial excerpt | The initial search excerpt covered movement only. Full second-pass reopening of S17 revealed a 10-coin matching reward. F25 was corrected to single-source, not left as an asserted absence. `together` deliberately retains unknown coin fields; `together-reported-bonus` exposes the conditional reported rule. The former is a conservative knowledge policy, not a claim that the game pays no bonus. No second independent observation or team-accounting confirmation was obtained. |
| C10 | Luigi probability and conditional faces | S20's approximate 33% activation claim is explicitly uncertain. Its comments also question whether an unmodified roll can naturally be ten. Mario and Luigi buddy mechanics are not selectable character dice. |
| C11 | Model fairness versus actual implementation | A list of possible outcomes does not prove equal weights or independence. F32 is an assumption. | C12 | Jamboree TV parity | S16/S21 document carried-over mechanics and new Together/Frenzy features. Availability is not a hardware retest of every old item, matching reward or interaction in TV. F33 remains partial. No general parity claim overrides this limit. |
| C13 | Mario composition versus direct observation | S19/S26 corroborate the 3–8 extra result; S07/S08 describe Payday contributions and S10/S19 the Creepy interaction. Other modeled item-plus-Mario combinations are explicitly conditional compositions of those rules, not independently observed scenarios. Mario's addend is applied once and excluded from the item's matching test. |
| C14 | Two algorithms versus two blind authors | Implementation A and implementation B use different algorithms and languages and are compared for every ordered tuple. The original production and Python reference were produced together. An additional TypeScript oracle was authored in a separate context from only the public contract and dice.json, sealed before production access, and compared against every production tuple and table. Its original seal and 41 self-check results are retained under tests/blind/. |
| C15 | Four-sigma acceptance is not a universal guarantee | Each selected seed is fixed in advance. Every reported bin must pass the exact four-sigma inequality. A future different seed may legitimately fail a per-bin threshold even with a correct sampler. Failed seeds must not be replaced. Exhaustive exact agreement is the principal correctness check. |

## Scope boundaries

This inventory addresses board-turn movement dice and dice-related items in the specified games. Coin gains before rolling from other buddies, board events, minigame dice props, starting-order/tie-break procedures, purchase costs, inventory acquisition probabilities and final coin caps are not included in the immediate roll reward. They are not secretly treated as zero or as independently verified. An all-modes physical-game capture audit was not performed.
