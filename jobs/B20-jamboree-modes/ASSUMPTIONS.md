# B20 — Assumptions and interpretation

- Editorial record names distinguish base versus TV Free Play/Party; they are not asserted as exact UI strings. Mode, submode, difficulty, rule variant and Buddy mechanic are separate classifications.
- Standard base Mario Party is outside this job. Its Pro Rules and Buddy mechanics are included as explicitly requested. Hub labels such as Motion Island, Bowser Challenges and Jamboree TV collect activities; they are not extra independent gameplay records.
- A two-source presence record certifies the listed activity, not its complete rules. Composite numeric rules remain single-source when a specific qualifier lacks independent evidence.
- Exact rules, reward rates, unlocks and timers remain unknown wherever no retained complete rule establishes them. Reported one-source numbers are labeled, not promoted by arithmetic or schema success.
- The phone/TV specs are original playable prototypes. Their dice, clocks, board layouts, physics, inputs, thresholds and tie rules are proposal values. They are not Nintendo-faithful reconstructions and do not imply empirical RNG weights.
- Original inputs work with touch controls. Sensor-like camera/microphone/motion interactions are adapted explicitly to touch actions; TV/server remains authoritative.
- Same-publisher pages count as one lineage. TheGamer articles by different authors do not count as independent corroboration; wiki redirects remain one lineage.
- Both research passes were conducted by the same assistant. Retrieval can be cached; hashes are of retrieved markdown. No origin-HTML, emulator, game-build or live gameplay observation is claimed.
