# Governing sources and fiction scope

The original B14 instructions are
https://github.com/luisitin/partybox-gpt-drops/blob/main/PROMPTS.md#b14-600-comedy-prompts--600-most-likely-to-r
and the repository rules are
https://github.com/luisitin/partybox-gpt-drops/blob/main/README.md.
Both were read before the resumed authoring and final verification work.

The artifact is original creative writing. All imagined situations, purported
labels, slogans, product behavior and celebrity dialogue are fictional, including
when they use a familiar cultural cue. They are not factual trivia or researched
claims about those real entities. There are zero nonfiction fact rows to pair
with research citations. No external quote, publisher attribution, retrieval
receipt, source-reopening pass or factual-source confidence rating is invented.

Actual verification consists of two fresh editorial grades for every candidate,
complete structural and similarity scans, and an adult/name audit of every final
row. Editorial confidence has a documented grading basis. If a future version
adds a nonfiction fact or explanatory historical note, it requires two genuinely
independent sources with actual <=25-word quotes and both source-opening passes
before publication; the current fiction scope cannot certify such an addition.
