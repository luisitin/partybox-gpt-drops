- Source facts describe Jamboree (2024) or the separately labeled Jamboree TV context. Older series rules are not silently imported.
- Mario Wiki pages, their aliases and the derived blueYOSHI tracker form one lineage. Nintendo pages form one lineage; Mario Party Legacy pages form one lineage.
- Short quotations plus section locators support a reported statement. Composite statements can remain single-source when one source omits a qualifier.
- Wiki voice/text transcriptions are exact to the retrieved source. They have not been matched against game frames; timestamp is null, punctuation and regional variants remain limited to that transcript.
- Homestretch's eight reported effects and wording about five options remain a conflict. Event sampling is null; uniformity is not inferred.
- Unknown bonus ties, eligibility thresholds and internal counters remain null or false. A confirmed core criterion does not certify all counter details.
- Exa may return cached markdown. Hashes fingerprint the retrieved text; they do not prove fresh-origin access or an installed game build.
- Both full audits were performed by the same assistant. No emulator, controlled gameplay replay, independent human researcher or exhaustive event-state test is claimed.
- Gameplay capture is useful follow-up evidence, not an additional requirement silently imposed on a source-attributed string.
