Music-credit questions distinguish original instrumental scores from soundtrack songs and lyricists: Godfather principal composer Nino Rota does not deny Carmine Coppola additional music; Wizard of Oz song music Harold Arlen does not misidentify Yip Harburg lyrics or Herbert Stothart score; Lion King score Hans Zimmer is separate from Elton John/Tim Rice songs; Sound of Music stage song composer Rodgers and original lyrics Hammerstein are separate from Kostal film arrangement.
