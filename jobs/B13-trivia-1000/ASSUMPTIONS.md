# B13 assumptions

- Ten broad categories in CONTRACT.md provide variety for US adult party players.
- Easy/medium/hard is a reasoned audience judgment; the target per category is
  34/33/33, for overall 340/330/330.
- Correct positions target exactly 25 of each per category, stricter than the
  original 25%±2% overall requirement.
- Every added factual clause in question and fun fact needs support in BOTH
  actual source quotations; a headline or unrelated page is insufficient.
- Source independence refers to authorship/editorial origin, not merely URL count.
- Reopening a shared page once in the second pass may support multiple row logs,
  but every row must be reassessed against that new retrieval and version hash.
- No current ranking, record holder, population, officeholder or evolving count
  is included without the original required explicit `as of 2025` cutoff.
- Full copyrighted web captures remain ignored local research files. Delivered
  excerpts stay brief and carry URLs/attribution; openly licensed materials retain
  their actual license notes.
